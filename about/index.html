About us

What is Pleasant Valley FIRST?

PVCSD FIRST is every student, mentor and faculty member who takes part in FIRST's robotics programs through the school. Robotics is more than building a robot. Students learn engineering alongside problem solving, teamwork and media skills they'll use every day.

Programs

What can you participate in?

FTC

FIRST Tech Challenge

Design, build, program and drive a robot in an alliance-style competition, and run the team behind it: fundraising, branding and outreach. Teams of up to fifteen PVCSD students in grades 9–12.

FRC

FIRST Robotics Competition

The same, at industrial scale: a bigger robot, a bigger team and large-team collaboration. Open to any number of PVCSD students in grades 9–12.
