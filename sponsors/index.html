Thank you

Our sponsors

Robots, registration fees, tools and travel all cost money. These partners make it possible for Pleasant Valley students to compete and grow.

Where support goes

Robot parts and kits, competition registration, shop tooling, travel to events, and outreach like summer camps and FLL mentoring.

What sponsors get

Recognition on our website, robots and team apparel, plus a direct connection to the next generation of engineers in the Quad Cities.

Ways to help

Financial sponsorship, in-kind donations of materials or machining, shop tours, or volunteering as a mentor.
