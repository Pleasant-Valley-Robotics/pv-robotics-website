STEM for students

More than building a robot.

The robotics programs at Pleasant Valley High School introduce young adults to science, technology, engineering and math. Students learn problem solving and the design process, along with communication, teamwork and presentation skills.

Our purpose

Skills, confidence, resilience.

The mission of FIRST is to provide life-changing robotics programs that give young people the skills, confidence and resilience to build a better world. PVCSD Robotics carries those values forward and connects students with mentors and the everyday applications of STEAM.
